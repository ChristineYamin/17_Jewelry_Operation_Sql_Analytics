# Jewlery Operations SQL Analytics

## Business Scenario
A Jewelry business operates multiple branches and sells different gold and jewelry products. Management currently has sales, customer, employee, product, supplier, inventory and gold-price records, but it cannot easily measure business performance or identify operational problems.

## Projecct Goal
Build a PostgreSQL Database and use SQL to analyse:
- Sales and revenue performance
- Customer purchasing behaviour
- Product and category performance
- Branch and employee performance
- Inventory shortages and slow-moving products
- Supplier performance
- Gold-price trends


## Stakeholders
- Business owner
- Branch Managers
- Sales Manager
- Inventory manager

## Key Business Questions
1. How are monthly sales and revenue changing?
2. which products and categories generate the most revenue?
3. Which branches perform best and worst?
4. Who are the most valuable customers?
5. Which employees generate the highest sales?
6. Which products are low in stock?
7. Which products are slow-moving?
8. Which suppliers provide the most products?
9. How do gold-price changes relate to sales?
10. What actions should management take?
